## High-RPM correction (v3)
F40 and Veneno use original procedural steady high-RPM loops above 5500/5700 RPM, crossfaded from the existing midrange. These are synthesized firing-order designs (V8/V12), not authentic manufacturer recordings. Generated by tools/prepare-high-rpm.mjs at a 6000 RPM reference; no ElevenLabs credits used for this correction. Linear complementary crossfade avoids a gain boost, and the high layer follows RPM without the previous 1.8x plateau.

## Four independent six-cylinder banks (v4)
Generated in ElevenLabs on 2026-09-14: eight 4-second groups, 160 credits each, total 1280 credits (87694 to 86414). Each car has separate 3500 RPM and 6200 RPM prompts (R34 high: 6500 RPM). Selected variants: 911 mid #2/high #3; R34 mid #4/high #1; Supra mid #2/high #3; M4 mid #1/high #2. Original filenames and SHA-256 processing audits are in tools/six-cylinder-sources.json and tools/six-cylinder-audio-audit.json.

These are AI-designed approximations of naturally aspirated Porsche boxer-six, Nissan RB26DETT, Toyota 2JZ-GTE and BMW S58 character, not manufacturer recordings or calibrated engine-order measurements. RPM anchors come from generation prompts. Selection uses visible waveform stability; authenticity has not been verified by listening. Low RPM retains separately shaped synthesis; medium/high RPM blend independently generated layers. The legacy six.wav bank is no longer loaded or used by any car. R34, Supra and M4 have separate lift-off gain/duration/rate settings.
