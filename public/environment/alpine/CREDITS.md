# Alpine scenery materials

Poly Haven assets, CC0: https://polyhaven.com/license

- Rock Face: https://polyhaven.com/a/rock_face
- Aerial Grass Rock: https://polyhaven.com/a/aerial_grass_rock
- Snow 02: https://polyhaven.com/a/snow_02
- Fir Tree 01 twig diffuse / alpha atlas: https://polyhaven.com/a/fir_tree_01 — Rico Cilliers / Rob Tuytel. The game's tree geometry is an original lightweight reconstruction, not the full source model.

Exact downloaded URLs and sizes: `sources.json`.

Landscape references: Red Bull Ring official circuit photographs, https://www.redbullring.com/en/track/red-bull-ring-f1-motogp-track/ ; Arosa tourism, https://arosalenzerheide.swiss/en/Arosa/Summer/Hiking . Reference photographs are not redistributed or used as in-game textures.

Mountain geometry, snow distribution, forest placement, boulders and lighting are artistic interpretations. Snow peaks and landscape scale are dramatized; this is not a surveyed digital twin. Road geometry and collision surfaces are unchanged by this scenery edition.

Coastal foliage: Poly Haven Island Tree 01 leaf diffuse and alpha textures (CC0), https://polyhaven.com/a/island_tree_01 . Downloaded 2026-09-15; used on original instanced tree crowns. These are coastal broadleaf visual references, not a botanical reconstruction of local eucalyptus species.
